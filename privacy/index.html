Privacy

What this website collects.

Short, because it collects very little.

Visiting

No cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising. Cloudflare serves the site and counts page visits with Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you.

Sending a form

We receive what you type: your name, email, company or property, role, room count, and any message. We also receive how you found us: the campaign tags on the link you followed, the first page you landed on, the site that sent you, and when. It goes to our inbox and to Attio, our CRM. We use it to reply to you about Kept or JQT. We never sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

On your device

Your browser keeps your own details and those campaign tags, so a second form does not ask twice. Nothing leaves your device until you send a form, and clearing this site's data removes it.

Deleting it

Write to hello@jqtenterprises.com and we will delete what we hold about you.
