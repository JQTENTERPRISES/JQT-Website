Questions

The things people ask first.

What does forward deployed actually mean?

It means we do not hand you a tool and disappear. We embed inside your operation, build the system that runs it, and stay responsible for whether it works in practice. The accountability does not transfer back to you on launch day.

Which industries do you work in?

Today we are deepest in hotels, aviation, property management, and field operations. The underlying system is one engine, so any operation that runs on status, deadlines, and handoffs is a fit. If you are not sure, ask.

Do we own the system, or rent it?

You own the core we build for your operation. It is shaped around how you actually work, not a generic template you lease forever and lose the day you stop paying.

How is it priced?

Priced to the value it creates, per operation, not a flat software fee. We scope what it saves and earns you, then price it as a fraction of that. You start with one operation and expand across your portfolio, and we size it openly with you on a call.

How fast can it be live?

Weeks, not months. We get one core piece of your operation running first, then build out while you are already using it. You see something real fast instead of waiting on one big launch.

How do you handle our data and security?

Your operation, your data, isolated to you and never pooled with anyone else's. Everything is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with AES 256. Access is per person with role based permissions, plus optional single sign on and two factor, so people only see what their job requires. Every action writes to an immutable audit log, which is also a feature you keep, and backups run automatically with point in time recovery. The AI assistant is sandboxed to only the data it is allowed to read, so it cannot leak or invent anything. Export or delete your data anytime. In regulated settings like aviation, we build to the record keeping standards your auditors already hold you to.
