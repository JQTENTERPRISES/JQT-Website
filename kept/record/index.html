The record

Who did what. Proven.

Every system in this category will hand you a report. A report is a claim, worth exactly as much as the vendor who printed it. Here is the difference between a report and a record, in three parts. You can check all three without taking our word for it, and the third one you can still check after we are gone.

01

The gate

Mark clean does not press until every step of the room checklist has been tapped. Not greyed out with an override for the manager. It does not press.

A room that is clean because someone said so from a chair at 4pm is the thing your current data is made of. This is the mechanic that ends it.

02

The chain

Every action appends to a log that cannot be quietly edited. Not by staff, not by the manager, not by us.

Each entry is built from the one before it, so altering an old line breaks every line after it, visibly, on a page your manager can open while you stand there.

03

The anchor

Once a day the head of that chain is sent to public RFC 3161 timestamp authorities, DigiCert and FreeTSA, and their signed reply is stored exactly as it came back.

The chain catches an edited row. The anchor catches the harder case: someone deletes the log and rebuilds a tidy fake whose hashes all recompute correctly. To fake history you would now also have to alter records held by a certificate authority.

And the proof outlives us. RFC 3161 is the same standard used to sign software and notarise documents. The reply is ordinary DER on your disk, so an auditor checks it years from now with openssl ts -verify, on a laptop, with no Kept account, no vendor, and no us. If we disappear, your record still stands up.

How an owner checks it in thirty seconds

Ask your manager to show you a room that ran over forty minutes last week, and what happened in it. A trashed checkout, a broken AC, a deep clean.

If the answer comes off the board while you are standing there, the record is alive. If it does not, then no software in your building is working, and that includes ours. It is a fair test either way, which is why it is the one we hand you.
